# What If baseline and hook tests

Goal: swipe-away under **33 %** on YouTube Shorts ("Stayed to watch" **at least 67 %**) and average percentage viewed **about 80 % or more**. These are working targets, not a promise of virality.

## Block T0: the first 10 "What if" videos

- Format stays **unchanged** for all 10 (see "Script – formula" in `CLAUDE.md`). No tests inside this block.
- Read every video **48 hours** after upload. Only YouTube counts (TikTok and Instagram show videos to followers first).
- Judge the block as a whole, never by one hit or one flop.
- After video 10: if most videos reach the targets, continue. If not, pick **one** rule to change and test it on the next 3 videos (keep it if the average improves by 5 points or more, drop it if it gets worse by 5 or more, otherwise keep the simpler option).
